Before introducing TEST/PRE-PROD/PROD, we need to understand the fundamental:

Plan → Approval → Apply

workflow.

We'll therefore add:

Terraform Plan - DEV
        ↓
Terraform Apply - DEV

with an approval mechanism between them.

Important concept

terraform plan is safe/read-only.

terraform plan

asks:

"What would happen if I applied my configuration?"

terraform apply actually changes Snowflake infrastructure.

terraform apply

asks:

"Make the real infrastructure match my Terraform configuration."

That's why production pipelines generally don't automatically jump from Plan → Apply.
